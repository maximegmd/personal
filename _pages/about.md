I work on **AI safety and alignment**: making frontier models honest about what they don't know, robust to adversarial manipulation, and safe to deploy where mistakes are costly. I'm also a medical doctor, and medicine has been my testbed. During my PhD in Computer Science at UCLouvain, I showed that [LLMs lack the metacognition needed for reliable medical reasoning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55628-6) and that [multiple-choice benchmarks reward pattern recognition over medical knowledge](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.266/). I also led the development of [Internist-7B](https://huggingface.co/internistai/base-7b-v0.2) and of an Epic-integrated clinical agent now used by 3,900+ clinicians at the Cliniques Universitaires Saint-Luc.

After working on reasoning-model distillation at Sophont (March–May 2026), I joined Jonathan Chen's lab at Stanford in August 2026 as an independent researcher, where I work on clinical data anonymization, synthetic data, and evaluations. In November 2026, I'm joining **Anthropic as an AI safety research fellow**. I'm especially interested in scalable oversight, honesty and calibration, adversarial robustness, and evaluations that measure what actually matters.
